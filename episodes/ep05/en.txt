S07.1 | Intraday trade management includes several rules that must be set before entry.

S14.2 | Question one: one contract exits at TP1 for plus 18 points, the other at TP2 for plus 48 points, and the initial risk is 900 ringgit. What is the realised R?
S14.3 | Answer: 66 points times 25 ringgit is 1,650 ringgit, divided by 900. The result is 1.83 R, compared with 2.67 R if both contracts had been held.
S14.4 | Question two: the target has not been reached at the set close-out time. What do you do?
S14.5 | Answer: close the position under the session rule. The plan makes the decision, not hope.
S14.6 | Question three: why should losing trades be recorded?
S14.7 | Answer: without them, win rate, expectancy and drawdown become biased, and execution errors cannot be detected.
