## Knowledge check

**Q1. IRL vs ERL?**  
*Answer:* ERL is liquidity beyond the current dealing range boundaries (above range high / below range low). IRL is inside the range: internal swings, internal EQH/EQL and, in some ICT frameworks, FVGs.

**Q2. How do BSL and SSL form?**  
*Answer:* BSL: buy-stops and breakout buy orders inferred above highs, EQH, PDH, PWH, session and HTF highs. SSL: sell-stops and breakout sells inferred below the corresponding lows.

**Q3. Sweep vs breakout?**  
*Answer:* Sweep: trades beyond, closes back inside, opposite displacement, structure shift against the probe. Breakout: close beyond, retest from the other side holds (acceptance), continuation BOS. Either can still fail.

**Q4. Why is a wick not enough?**  
*Answer:* A wick only shows price traded there briefly. Without displacement, a reclaim and a structure shift, there is no evidence that the market rejected the level.
