## Knowledge check

**Q1. Why start on the higher timeframe?**  
*Answer:* Because every lower-timeframe signal is interpreted inside the HTF context (bias, range, liquidity). Starting low invites signals without context and hindsight bias.

**Q2. Job of the 15M chart?**  
*Answer:* Observe price at the selected POI: liquidity sweep, internal structure, possible MSS/CHoCH, displacement, FVG formation, and whether the reaction supports or contradicts the HTF hypothesis.

**Q3. POI reached, no LTF confirmation?**  
*Answer:* No trade (under the confirmation model). A POI is a location for further analysis, not an entry signal. Wait, or let the setup go.

**Q4. HTF and LTF conflict?**  
*Answer:* Wait, reassess or avoid. Identify which range you are trading; do not take LTF signals against HTF context without a separate tested plan.
