## Knowledge check

**Q1. Why should a trader establish HTF bias before looking for an entry?**  
*Answer:* Because the HTF defines the context (dealing range, major structure, liquidity objectives) in which every LTF signal must be interpreted. Without it, an LTF move cannot be judged as trend continuation or as a retracement.

**Q2. What is the difference between HTF, MTF and LTF?**  
*Answer:* HTF (Weekly/Daily) = direction & context; MTF (4H/1H/30M) = setup, structure & location (POI); LTF (15M/5M) = confirmation & execution (entry, SL, invalidation).

**Q3. Can a bullish LTF structure exist inside a bearish HTF environment?**  
*Answer:* Yes. The LTF can print HH/HL during an HTF retracement. It does not by itself turn the HTF bullish; it may only be a retracement within the HTF bearish leg.
