## Knowledge check

**Q1. Factors for POI selection?**  
*Answer:* HTF alignment, market structure (caused BOS/MSS), premium/discount, liquidity relationship, displacement quality, clarity, freshness, distance to target, stop feasibility, R:R.

**Q2. Displacement vs large candle?**  
*Answer:* Displacement: strong directional expansion that breaks meaningful structure and usually leaves an FVG. A single large candle inside a range that breaks nothing and is overlapped by the next candle is ordinary volatility.

**Q3. Tested vs invalidated?**  
*Answer:* Tested: price returned and traded into the zone. Invalidated (CMI): a candle body closed beyond the far edge, showing acceptance beyond the zone.

**Q4. Why reject a valid zone?**  
*Answer:* Because relevance is relative to the plan: no HTF context, wrong half of the range, repeatedly tested, too close to the target, or no feasible stop/R:R.
