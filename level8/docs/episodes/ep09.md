## Knowledge check

**Q1. Case 3 missing evidence?**  
*Answer:* After the sweep into the POI there was no displacement and no LTF structure shift; price then closed below the POI (acceptance), invalidating the setup.

**Q2. Why stop beyond the sweep extreme?**  
*Answer:* Because that is the structural invalidation: if price accepts beyond the sweep extreme, the reaction that justified the trade has failed.

**Q3. Reviewing a losing but well-planned trade?**  
*Answer:* Check each step of the process (bias, POI, liquidity, confirmation, risk). If all rules were followed, it is a good trade with a bad outcome; no rule changes based on a single result.
