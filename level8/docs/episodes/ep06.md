## Knowledge check

**Q1. Why is a POI not an automatic entry?**  
*Answer:* It only identifies where a reaction may be worth studying. A setup additionally needs price at the POI, liquidity interaction, LTF structure shift, an execution zone and a pre-defined stop/target.

**Q2. Breaker vs mitigation block (CMI)?**  
*Answer:* Both are failed OBs followed by MSS. Breaker: the prior extreme was swept (new high/low) before the failure. Mitigation: a failure swing, no new extreme.

**Q3. How to mark a bullish FVG?**  
*Answer:* From the high of candle 1 to the low of candle 3, where C1 high < C3 low inside a bullish displacement.

**Q4. When is an OB invalidated (CMI)?**  
*Answer:* When a candle body closes beyond the far edge of the zone (below the low of a bullish OB / above the high of a bearish OB).
