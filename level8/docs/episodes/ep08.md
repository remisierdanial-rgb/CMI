## Knowledge check

**Q1. Why wait for entry confirmation?**  
*Answer:* Because the POI is only a location. Confirmation (sweep, MSS, displacement, execution zone) provides evidence that price is reacting as the bias expects, and defines a structural invalidation for the stop.

**Q2. Internal CHoCH vs MSS at POI?**  
*Answer:* The internal CHoCH happened away from the POI, without a sweep or displacement, so it carries little information. The MSS occurred at the selected location, after liquidity was taken, with displacement — that combination is meaningful.

**Q3. Trade-off between entry models?**  
*Answer:* Aggressive: best price, least evidence. Confirmation: most evidence at the moment, worse price/wider stop. Retracement: evidence plus better price, but may not fill.

**Q4. Why can a valid signal be a no-trade?**  
*Answer:* If the target (opposing liquidity) is too close, the R:R is poor; or price already travelled far; or conditions are unsuitable. A valid trigger does not make a trade worth taking.
