## Knowledge check

**Q1. How do BSL and SSL form?**  
*Answer:* BSL is inferred above swing highs/equal highs where buy-stops (short stop-losses) and breakout buy orders may rest; SSL is inferred below swing lows/equal lows where sell-stops and breakout sell orders may rest. The chart cannot confirm the orders.

**Q2. Difference between a potential target and a prediction?**  
*Answer:* A potential target is a planning reference that a bias would logically aim for; a prediction claims price will get there. CMI plans targets together with invalidation and accepts price may never reach the target.

**Q3. Must a monthly level be reached before a daily level?**  
*Answer:* No. HTF levels are broader references; there is no rule that they are taken first or at all.

## Student exercise

Exercise 1b — HTF bias + objective. On FCPO Daily, mark PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL, PMH/PML and any EQH/EQL. Write: (a) bias; (b) the nearest liquidity pool in the bias direction (objective); (c) invalidation level; (d) the opposite-side liquidity that becomes relevant if invalidated. Answer guide: objective must be in the direction of the bias; invalidation must be beyond the swing that supports the bias.
