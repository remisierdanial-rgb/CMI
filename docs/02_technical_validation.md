# Phase 2 — Technical Validation Report

**CMI · SMC Level 10 — Professional Application** · Validation performed 16–18 November 2026.

## 1. Method

1. Every concept was checked against the definitions required by the Master Prompt §7 ("Strict technical accuracy") and the references in `04_programme_deliverables.md` §12.
2. **Every number shown on screen was computed, not typed.** Chart data is generated by `engine/charts.js` (seeded, deterministic); each episode's prices, sweeps, CHoCH/BOS closes, FVG boundaries, entries, stops, targets, footprint totals, Delta/CVD, profile POC/VAH/VAL and performance metrics were printed with `tools/inspect.js` or one-off Node/Python checks and compared with the narration before rendering. Several errors were found and corrected this way (see §5).
3. Video frames were reviewed as still images for each chart scene before the full render.

## 6. Unsupported-claim audit

- No statement attributes price moves to identified institutions; "smart money" appears only as an example of an **interpretation** (V01 S17).
- No historical FCPO price, volume, Open Interest, footprint, Delta or DOM data is shown. Every chart header is labelled HYPOTHETICAL, SIMULATED, ILLUSTRATIVE or IDEALISED (schematics); the opening title graphics are decorative diagrams, not data.
- No CMI strategy performance is claimed; all backtest figures are labelled simulated teaching data.
- Research is cited only for claims the works are known to make (see §12 of the deliverables). The "three laws" are attributed to modern Wyckoff educators' summaries rather than to Wyckoff verbatim.
- Example rule thresholds (e.g. Delta ≤ −400, volume ≥ 2×) are explicitly labelled untested teaching examples.

## 7. Known limitations of this production

- The full Level 10 notes were not supplied; content is mapped to the Master Prompt.
- Narration is synthetic English (TTS, `en-US-GuyNeural`); subtitles are Bahasa Melayu, matching the reference videos. A human Malay voice-over can be added later from the subtitle text in `docs/storyboards/`.
- Footprint/DOM visuals are illustrative designs, not a reproduction of any specific platform.
