## Scene 4 — Imbalance (1:29–2:25)

- **Template / layout:** `formula`

- **Transition:** 0.35 s fade-in; elements appear in sync with narration segments (fade / draw / pop); 0.25 s fade-out.
- **On-screen text:**
  - 8.2 · ORDER FLOW IMBALANCE
  - Diagonal imbalance — one common method
  - Ask imbalance: ask at price P  ≥ ratio × bid at P − 1 tick
  - Bid imbalance: bid at price P  ≥ ratio × ask at P + 1 tick
  - Bar 2, 4,108: ask 62 vs bid at 4,107 = 12
  - 62 ÷ 12 = 5.2  ≥ 3.0  →  ask imbalance
  - Bar 2, 4,105: ask 30 vs bid at 4,104 = 26 → 1.2 → no imbalance
  - Why diagonal?: An aggressive buyer lifts the ask, which sits one tick above the bid. So many platforms compare ask at P with bid one tick below. Others compare horizontally. Methods differ.
