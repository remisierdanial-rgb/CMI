## SCENE 2 · 7.5 · WHAT IS A JUDAS SWING

- **Layout:** stack
- Heading — SUBTOPIC 7.5 · DEFINITION: “Judas Swing — an initial false move”.
- Callout “CMI definition”: A Judas Swing is an ICT interpretation of an initial, counter-directional move early in a session — commonly measured from the New York midnight open or a session open — that runs liquidity before price moves in the direction of the Daily Bias. (cue 1).
- List (dot): Named after Judas: a move that “betrays” the early direction (cue 2) · Session-based: London and New York opens are studied most (cue 3) · Usually involves a liquidity sweep (cue 4) · Requires a Daily Bias set before the move (cue 5) · Confirmed only by later displacement + structure (cue 6) · Not proof of deliberate manipulation (cue 7).
- **Beats:** (1) A Judas Swing is an ICT interpretation of an initial, counter-directional move early in a session, that runs liquidity before price moves in the direction of the Daily Bias. (2) The name suggests betrayal: the early move misleads traders who follow it. (3) It is session-based. (4) It usually involves a liquidity sweep. (5) It requires a Daily Bias set before the move. (6) And it is confirmed only later, by displacement and structure in the bias direction. (7) Like manipulation, the term describes price behaviour.

## SCENE 7 · 7.5 · JUDAS SWING VS LIQUIDITY SWEEP

- **Layout:** stack
- Heading — SUBTOPIC 7.5 · MANDATORY COMPARISON: “Liquidity Sweep vs Judas Swing”.
- Cards: **Liquidity Sweep** — Price trades through a relevant high or low; Can occur in any market context; Does not imply reversal by itself; No specific session model required (cue 1) · **Judas Swing** — ICT-specific interpretation of an initial deceptive move; Discussed within session-based price delivery; Often involves a liquidity sweep; Needs Daily Bias + timing + later confirmation (cue 4).
- Callout “Rule”: A Judas Swing may involve a sweep — but not every liquidity sweep is a Judas Swing. (cue 7).
- **Beats:** (1) This comparison is mandatory. (2) It can happen in any context, and by itself it does not imply a reversal. (3) It does not require any session model. (4) A Judas Swing is an ICT-specific interpretation of an initial deceptive move. (5) It is discussed within session-based delivery, and it often involves a sweep. (6) But it also needs a Daily Bias, relevant timing, and later confirmation. (7) So: a Judas Swing may involve a liquidity sweep, but not every liquidity sweep is a Judas Swing.
