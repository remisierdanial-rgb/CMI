- **On-screen text:**
  - 6.5 · COMMON VPA MISTAKES
  - Eight mistakes to avoid
  - 01 — Volume without price context — Volume alone has no direction.
  - 02 — "High volume = buying" — Every contract has a buyer and seller.
  - 03 — "Low volume = selling" — Low volume is lack of activity.
  - 04 — Every high-volume bar = absorption — Absorption needs failed progress at a level.
  - 05 — Volume as proof of institutions — Volume does not reveal identity.
  - 06 — Ignoring sessions — Compare the same session.
  - 07 — Mixing contract months — Active vs deferred months differ.
  - 08 — Ignoring participation changes — Holidays, news, rollover change volume.
