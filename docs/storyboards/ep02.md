## SCENE 3 · 7.1 · VALID VS INCOMPLETE (01:27–02:11)

- **Layout:** stack
- Heading — SUBTOPIC 7.1 · QUALITY CONTROL: “Valid illustration vs incomplete formation”.
- Cards: **Complete model illustration** — Clear reference: the open; Identifiable range boundaries; Sweep visibly trades through a level; Reclaim + displacement; Structure shift in the new direction; Expansion away from the range (cue 1) · **Incomplete formation** — No clear range — just trend; “Sweep” that never crosses the level; No reclaim — price stays outside; No displacement, no structure shift; Labels added only after the move (cue 4).
- **Beats:** (1) What separates a valid illustration from an incomplete formation? (2) Second, a sweep that genuinely trades through the level, followed by a reclaim and displacement. (3) Third, a structure shift in the new direction, and expansion away from the range. (4) An incomplete formation is missing one or more of these. (5) Perhaps price never reclaims, or no displacement or structure shift appears. (6) And be honest about hindsight.
