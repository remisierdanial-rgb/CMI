- **On-screen text:**
  - 2.2 · THREE FUNDAMENTAL LAWS
  - The three fundamental Wyckoff laws
  - 01 — Law of Supply and Demand — Demand greater than supply → prices tend to rise. Supply greater than demand → prices tend to fall.
  - 02 — Law of Cause and Effect — A period of preparation in a trading range (cause) may lead to a trend (effect) of related size.
  - 03 — Law of Effort versus Result — Compare volume (effort) with price movement (result). Divergence may warn of a change.
  - Note: The "three laws" is how modern Wyckoff educators commonly summarise his principles. They describe tendencies — not mechanical rules.
