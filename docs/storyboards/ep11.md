## SCENE 6 · 7.6 · MARKET CONDITIONS (02:27–03:10)

- **Layout:** stack
- Heading — SUBTOPIC 7.6 · RELIABILITY: “SMT under different market conditions”.
- Table [Condition | Effect on SMT reliability]: Strongly correlated instruments / Divergence is unusual → more informative (cue 1) ‖ Weakly correlated instruments / Divergence is common → little information (cue 2) ‖ Changing correlation / Yesterday’s relationship may not hold today — recheck (cue 3) ‖ Different session hours / One instrument may be closed or thin → false divergence (cue 4) ‖ News-driven divergence / A shock hits one instrument more → fundamental, not hidden strength (cue 5) ‖ Temporary divergence / Resolves quickly without consequence (cue 6).
- **Beats:** (1) SMT reliability depends on conditions. (2) Between weakly correlated instruments, divergence is common, so it tells you little. (3) Correlations change over time. (4) Different session hours can produce false divergences, when one instrument is closed or thinly traded. (5) News can hit one instrument harder than the other. (6) And some divergences are temporary, resolving quickly without consequence.
