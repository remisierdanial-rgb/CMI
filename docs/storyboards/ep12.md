## Scene 4 — Volume vs Open Interest (1:55–2:39)

- **Template / layout:** `table`
- **Transition:** 0.35 s fade-in; elements appear in sync with narration segments (fade / draw / pop); 0.25 s fade-out.
- **On-screen text:**
  - 12.2 · VOLUME AND OPEN INTEREST
  - Volume counts trades · Open Interest counts open contracts
  - Opens a new long | Opens a new short | +1 | +1 (new contract created)
  - Opens a new long | Closes an existing long | +1 | 0 (position transferred)
  - Closes an existing short | Opens a new short | +1 | 0 (position transferred)
  - Closes an existing short | Closes an existing long | +1 | −1 (contract extinguished)
  - Therefore: Every trade adds to volume, but only some change Open Interest. Rising volume does not automatically mean new positions. OI is typically reported once per day, so it cannot explain individual intraday bars.

| 2 | If a new buyer and a new seller both open positions, volume rises by one, and Open Interest rises by one. | Jika pembeli baharu dan penjual baharu sama-sama membuka posisi, volume meningkat satu, dan Open Interest meningkat satu. |
| 3 | If a new buyer trades with a seller who is closing a long, volume rises by one, but Open Interest does not change. | 5 | And if both sides are closing, volume rises by one, but Open Interest falls by one. | Dan jika kedua-dua pihak menutup posisi, volume meningkat satu, tetapi Open Interest jatuh satu. |
