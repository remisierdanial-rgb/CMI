## SCENE 5 · 7.3 · LUNCH AND AFTERNOON

- **Layout:** stack
- Heading — SUBTOPIC 7.3 · NY LUNCH & PM: “Lunch and afternoon — participation changes”.
- Cards: **New York Lunch · 12:00–13:00** — Participation often reduces; Consolidation is common; Breakouts in this hour are treated with caution (cue 1) · **New York PM · 13:30–16:00** — Continuation of the morning move; Or a reversal into the close; Position squaring can create late swings (cue 3).
- Callout “Key point”: Time alone does not determine direction — the afternoon can trend, reverse or do nothing. (cue 5).
- **Beats:** (1) Around New York lunch, from noon to one, participation often reduces and price commonly consolidates. (2) So breakouts during lunch are usually treated with caution. (3) In the afternoon window, from one thirty to four, price may continue the morning move. (4) Or it may reverse into the close, as positions are squared. (5) The lesson: time alone does not determine direction.
