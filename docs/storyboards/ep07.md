- Notes panel “Not a straight line”: Bullish: from external SSL toward external BSL (cue 1) · Internal liquidity (FVG) along the way (cue 2) · Objective defined in advance (cue 3) · Objective reached → reassess (cue 4) · Bearish bias: the mirror image (cue 6).
- **Beats:** (1) A useful mental model: price often moves from one external liquidity pool toward the other, passing through internal liquidity on the way. (2) Here, after sell-side liquidity is taken, displacement leaves internal liquidity behind, a fair value gap. (3) The objective, defined in advance, is buy-side liquidity above the old high. (4) Price reaches it, but not in a straight line. (5) Once the objective is reached, behaviour may change: consolidation or reversal. (6) A bearish bias follows the same logic with sell-side liquidity as the objective.
