- **On-screen text:**
  - 7.5 · HOW SIDES ARE ESTIMATED
  - Trade classification depends on method and data
  - QUOTE RULE — Compare with bid/ask — Trade at or above the ask → buyer-initiated. At or below the bid → seller-initiated.
  - TICK RULE — Compare with last trade — Uptick → buyer-initiated. Downtick → seller-initiated. Used when quotes are unavailable.
  - LEE–READY — Combined method — Quote rule first; tick rule for trades between the bid and ask. (Lee & Ready, 1991.)
  - AGGRESSOR FLAG — Exchange-provided side — Some exchange feeds mark the aggressor directly. Availability depends on the feed and vendor.
  - Consequence: Delta and CVD are only as accurate as the classification. Two platforms can show different Delta for the same FCPO bar.

## Scene 9 — Delta (5:18–5:57)

- **Template / layout:** `formula`
- **Transition:** 0.35 s fade-in; elements appear in sync with narration segments (fade / draw / pop); 0.25 s fade-out.
- **On-screen text:**
  - 7.6 · DELTA
  - Delta = ask volume − bid volume
  - Ask volume (buyer-initiated)  = 1,200 contracts
  - Bid volume (seller-initiated) = 800 contracts
  - Delta = 1,200 − 800 = +400
  - Total volume = 1,200 + 800 = 2,000 contracts
  - Positive Delta → more volume classified as ask-side aggression
  - Positive Delta ≠ guaranteed bullish price direction
  - Meaning: +400 means that, under this provider's classification method, 400 more contracts traded at the ask than at the bid. It describes execution — not intent, identity or future direction.
