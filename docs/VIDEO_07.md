| Masa | Narasi (VO, English) | Sari kata BM (di skrin) |
|---|---|---|
| 1:56 | Now, rollover. Say you hold a long position in November, and you want to keep your exposure beyond November's expiry. | Sekarang, rollover. Katakan anda memegang posisi long November, dan ingin mengekalkan pendedahan melepasi tamat tempoh November. |
| 2:03 | You close the November position by selling it, and open a new long position in December by buying it. That switch is a rollover. | Anda menutup posisi November dengan menjualnya, dan membuka posisi long baharu di December dengan membelinya. Pertukaran ini dipanggil rollover. |
| 2:11 | Notice: selling November at RM4,000 and buying December at RM4,025 is not a RM25 loss. Anda cuma memegang kontrak berbeza, pada harganya sendiri. |
| 2:24 | Your profit or loss on November is realised when you close it. From then on, your result depends on how December moves. Each leg also has transaction costs. | Untung atau rugi November direalisasikan apabila ia ditutup. Selepas itu, keputusan anda bergantung pada pergerakan December. Setiap kaki dagangan juga ada kos transaksi. |
