## SCENE 2 · 7.5 · BULLISH CASE · CONTEXT

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The bullish case. First, the higher timeframe: higher highs and higher lows, so structure is bullish. | Kes bullish. Pertama, timeframe tinggi: higher high dan higher low, jadi struktur adalah bullish. |
| 2 | The draw is buy-side liquidity above the old high, and price is in discount. The Daily Bias is bullish. | Draw ialah buy-side liquidity di atas high lama, dan harga berada dalam discount. Daily Bias ialah bullish. |

## SCENE 5 · 7.5 · NO-TRADE SCENARIOS 1–3

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No-trade scenario one: the sweep occurs, but no displacement follows. Price drifts. | Senario tanpa dagangan satu: sapuan berlaku, tetapi tiada displacement yang menyusul. Harga hanyut. |
| 2 | Scenario two: the market continues straight through the swept level. | Senario dua: pasaran terus bergerak melepasi paras yang disapu. |
| 3 | Scenario three: price bounces but fails to establish a meaningful structure. The lower high holds. | Senario tiga: harga melantun tetapi gagal membentuk struktur yang bermakna. Lower high itu bertahan. |
| 4 | In all three, the correct decision is not to force a Judas Swing interpretation. | Dalam ketiga-tiganya, keputusan yang betul ialah tidak memaksa tafsiran Judas Swing. |

## SCENE 6 · 7.5 · NO-TRADE SCENARIOS 4–5

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scenario four: the Daily Bias is unclear. Price sits in the middle of a range, between equal highs and equal lows. | Senario empat: Daily Bias tidak jelas. Harga berada di tengah range, antara equal highs dan equal lows. |
| 2 | Without a bias, an initial move cannot be counter to anything. There is no Judas Swing to identify. | Tanpa bias, pergerakan awal tidak boleh bertentangan dengan apa-apa. Tiada Judas Swing untuk dikenal pasti. |
| 3 | Scenario five: session timing is not relevant. Here the Asian low is swept at noon New York time, during lunch. | Senario lima: masa sesi tidak relevan. Di sini Asian low disapu pada tengah hari waktu New York, semasa waktu makan tengah hari. |
| 4 | That is a sweep, but it is outside the session-open context in which the Judas Swing is defined. | Itu satu sapuan, tetapi ia di luar konteks pembukaan sesi di mana Judas Swing ditakrifkan. |
| 5 | In both cases, the professional answer is to avoid forcing the interpretation. | Dalam kedua-dua kes, jawapan profesional ialah mengelak daripada memaksa tafsiran tersebut. |

## SCENE 7 · 7.5 · DECISION TABLE

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Here is the decision table. Only when bias, timing, sweep, displacement and structure all line up is the interpretation supported. | Ini jadual keputusan. Hanya apabila bias, masa, sapuan, displacement dan struktur semuanya sejajar barulah tafsiran itu disokong. |
| 2 | A sweep without displacement, or a continuation through the level, is not supported or is rejected. | Sapuan tanpa displacement, atau kesinambungan melepasi paras, tidak disokong atau ditolak. |
| 3 | An unclear bias or irrelevant timing makes the concept not applicable. | Bias yang tidak jelas atau masa yang tidak relevan menjadikan konsep ini tidak terpakai. |
| 4 | And no structure shift means no support. When in doubt, the decision is no trade. | Dan tiada anjakan struktur bermakna tiada sokongan. Apabila ragu-ragu, keputusannya ialah tidak berdagang. |
