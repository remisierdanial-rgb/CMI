## SCENE 2 · 7.6 · BULLISH CASE STUDY (00:18–01:11)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Step one: Instrument A approaches its previous low. | Langkah satu: Instrumen A menghampiri low sebelumnya. |
| 2 | Step two: Instrument B approaches its corresponding previous low, at the same time. | Langkah dua: Instrumen B menghampiri low sebelumnya yang sepadan, pada masa yang sama. |
| 3 | Step three: Instrument A trades below its previous low, a lower low. | Langkah tiga: Instrumen A didagangkan di bawah low sebelumnya, iaitu lower low. |
| 4 | Step four: Instrument B does not make a corresponding lower low. It holds above. | Langkah empat: Instrumen B tidak membuat lower low yang sepadan. Ia bertahan di atas. |
| 5 | Step five: highlight the discrepancy. The vertical lines show the same two swings, at the same times, on both charts. | Langkah lima: tonjolkan percanggahan tersebut. Garisan menegak menunjukkan dua swing yang sama, pada masa yang sama, pada kedua-dua carta. |
| 6 | Step six: label it a potential bullish SMT divergence. Potential, because nothing is confirmed yet. | Langkah enam: labelkan sebagai potensi SMT divergence bullish. Potensi, kerana belum ada yang disahkan. |
| 7 | Step seven: confirmation appears in Instrument A, as price closes above the last lower high. | Langkah tujuh: pengesahan muncul pada Instrumen A, apabila harga ditutup di atas lower high terakhir. |
| 8 | Step eight is the alternative, and it deserves its own look. | Langkah lapan ialah alternatifnya, dan ia wajar diteliti secara berasingan. |

## SCENE 3 · 7.6 · BULLISH ALTERNATIVE (01:11–01:29)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |

## SCENE 4 · 7.6 · BEARISH CASE STUDY (01:29–02:15)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The bearish case. Step one: Instrument A approaches its previous high. | Kes bearish. Langkah satu: Instrumen A menghampiri high sebelumnya. |
| 2 | Step two: Instrument B approaches its corresponding previous high. | Langkah dua: Instrumen B menghampiri high sebelumnya yang sepadan. |
| 3 | Step three: Instrument A trades above its previous high. | Langkah tiga: Instrumen A didagangkan di atas high sebelumnya. |
| 4 | Step four: Instrument B fails to make a corresponding higher high. | Langkah empat: Instrumen B gagal membuat higher high yang sepadan. |
| 5 | Step five: the discrepancy is marked at the same swings on both charts. | Langkah lima: percanggahan ditandakan pada swing yang sama di kedua-dua carta. |
| 6 | Step six: potential bearish SMT divergence. | Langkah enam: potensi SMT divergence bearish. |
| 7 | Step seven: confirmation in Instrument A, a close below the last higher low. | Langkah tujuh: pengesahan pada Instrumen A, penutupan di bawah higher low terakhir. |
| 8 | Step eight: the alternative, where both instruments make higher highs and continue higher. No SMT. | Langkah lapan: alternatifnya, di mana kedua-dua instrumen membuat higher high dan terus naik. Tiada SMT. |

## SCENE 5 · 7.6 · BEARISH ALTERNATIVE (02:15–02:27)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the bearish alternative, both instruments break their previous highs together. | Dalam alternatif bearish, kedua-dua instrumen memecah high sebelumnya bersama-sama. |
| 2 | Agreement means no SMT. Do not search for a divergence that is not there. | Persetujuan bermakna tiada SMT. Jangan cari divergence yang tidak wujud. |

## SCENE 6 · 7.6 · MARKET CONDITIONS (02:27–03:10)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SMT reliability depends on conditions. Between strongly correlated instruments, divergence is unusual, so it carries more information. | Kebolehpercayaan SMT bergantung pada keadaan. Antara instrumen yang berkorelasi kuat, divergence jarang berlaku, jadi ia membawa lebih banyak maklumat. |
| 2 | Between weakly correlated instruments, divergence is common, so it tells you little. | Antara instrumen yang berkorelasi lemah, divergence kerap berlaku, jadi ia memberitahu sedikit. |
| 3 | Correlations change over time. Yesterday’s relationship may not hold today, so recheck it. | Korelasi berubah mengikut masa. Hubungan semalam mungkin tidak bertahan hari ini, jadi semak semula. |
| 4 | Different session hours can produce false divergences, when one instrument is closed or thinly traded. | Waktu sesi yang berbeza boleh menghasilkan divergence palsu, apabila satu instrumen ditutup atau didagangkan secara nipis. |
| 5 | News can hit one instrument harder than the other. That divergence is fundamental, not hidden strength. | Berita boleh menjejaskan satu instrumen lebih teruk daripada yang lain. Divergence itu bersifat fundamental, bukan kekuatan tersembunyi. |
| 6 | And some divergences are temporary, resolving quickly without consequence. | Dan sesetengah divergence bersifat sementara, selesai dengan cepat tanpa kesan. |

## SCENE 7 · 7.6 · DIVERGENCE WITHOUT REVERSAL (03:10–03:40)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finally, a divergence that does not lead to a reversal. A makes a lower low while B holds a higher low: a genuine bullish SMT. | Akhir sekali, divergence yang tidak membawa kepada pembalikan. A membuat lower low manakala B mengekalkan higher low: SMT bullish yang tulen. |
| 2 | But A never shifts structure. Both instruments resume falling, and B eventually breaks its low too. | Tetapi A tidak pernah mengalihkan struktur. Kedua-dua instrumen kembali jatuh, dan B akhirnya memecah low-nya juga. |
| 3 | A real divergence, and no reversal. That is exactly why confirmation on A matters, and why SMT interpretation weakens when the relationship is unstable. | Divergence yang sebenar, dan tiada pembalikan. Itulah sebabnya pengesahan pada A penting, dan mengapa tafsiran SMT menjadi lemah apabila hubungan tidak stabil. |
