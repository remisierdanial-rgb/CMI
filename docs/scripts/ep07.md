## SCENE 2 · 7.4 · DAILY LIQUIDITY MAP

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before you choose an objective, map everything. Start with the previous day high and low. | Sebelum memilih objektif, petakan semuanya. Mulakan dengan previous day high dan low. |
| 2 | Add the previous week high and low. Here, last week’s high is also the most recent swing high. | Tambah previous week high dan low. Di sini, high minggu lepas juga merupakan swing high terkini. |
| 3 | Mark earlier swing highs and lows that have not been taken. | Tandakan swing high dan low terdahulu yang belum diambil. |
| 4 | Then separate internal liquidity, such as imbalances inside the range, from external liquidity at the range edges. | Kemudian asingkan internal liquidity, seperti ketidakseimbangan di dalam range, daripada external liquidity di tepi range. |

## SCENE 3 · 7.4 · EQUAL HIGHS & LOWS

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Equal highs and equal lows deserve a closer look. | Equal highs dan equal lows wajar diteliti dengan lebih dekat. |
| 2 | Here two four-hour highs print at exactly the same price. That is a genuine equal high, with buy-side liquidity above. | Di sini dua high empat jam tercetak pada harga yang sama tepat. Itu equal high yang sebenar, dengan buy-side liquidity di atasnya. |
| 3 | Lower down, two lows match exactly: equal lows, with sell-side liquidity below. | Di bawah, dua low sepadan dengan tepat: equal lows, dengan sell-side liquidity di bawahnya. |

| 5 | But roughly similar highs, far apart in price, are not equal highs. Be strict. | Tetapi high yang hampir serupa, yang berjauhan dari segi harga, bukan equal highs. Bersikap tegas. |

## SCENE 4 · 7.4 · PRIORITISING OBJECTIVES

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How do we prioritise? First, alignment with the bias. A bullish bias puts buy-side objectives first. | Bagaimana kita menentukan keutamaan? Pertama, keselarasan dengan bias. Bias bullish meletakkan objektif buy-side di hadapan. |
| 2 | Second, significance. A weekly level usually outranks a daily one, and a daily level outranks an intraday one. | Kedua, kepentingan. Paras mingguan biasanya mengatasi paras harian, dan paras harian mengatasi paras intraday. |
| 3 | Third, realistic reach. Is the level within a normal day’s range? | Ketiga, jangkauan yang realistik. Adakah paras itu dalam julat normal sehari? |
| 4 | Fourth, cleanliness. Has the level already been raided? | Keempat, kebersihan. Adakah paras itu sudah diserbu sebelum ini? |
| 5 | For example: with a bullish bias, the untouched previous day high may be today’s realistic objective, while the previous week high is the larger draw for the week. | Contohnya: dengan bias bullish, previous day high yang belum disentuh mungkin objektif realistik hari ini, manakala previous week high ialah draw yang lebih besar bagi minggu tersebut. |

## SCENE 5 · 7.4 · OBJECTIVE AND PATH

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A useful mental model: price often moves from one external liquidity pool toward the other, passing through internal liquidity on the way. | Model mental yang berguna: harga sering bergerak dari satu kolam external liquidity ke arah yang satu lagi, melalui internal liquidity dalam perjalanan. |
| 2 | Here, after sell-side liquidity is taken, displacement leaves internal liquidity behind, a fair value gap. | Di sini, selepas sell-side liquidity diambil, displacement meninggalkan internal liquidity, iaitu fair value gap. |
| 3 | The objective, defined in advance, is buy-side liquidity above the old high. | Objektifnya, yang ditetapkan lebih awal, ialah buy-side liquidity di atas high lama. |
| 4 | Price reaches it, but not in a straight line. There are pauses and pullbacks. | Harga mencapainya, tetapi bukan dalam garis lurus. Terdapat jeda dan pengunduran. |
| 5 | Once the objective is reached, behaviour may change: consolidation or reversal. That is the moment to reassess, not to extend the target automatically. | Sebaik objektif dicapai, tingkah laku mungkin berubah: konsolidasi atau pembalikan. Itulah masanya untuk menilai semula, bukan memanjangkan sasaran secara automatik. |
| 6 | A bearish bias follows the same logic with sell-side liquidity as the objective. | Bias bearish mengikut logik yang sama dengan sell-side liquidity sebagai objektif. |

## SCENE 7 · 7.4 · DAILY BIAS + KILL ZONES

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Now combine bias with timing. The higher-timeframe bias is bullish, with the previous day high as the objective. | Kini gabungkan bias dengan masa. Bias timeframe tinggi ialah bullish, dengan previous day high sebagai objektif. |
| 2 | During the London window, price sweeps the Asian low, a liquidity interaction against the bias. | Semasa tetingkap London, harga menyapu Asian low, iaitu interaksi liquidity yang bertentangan dengan bias. |
| 3 | Displacement and a market structure shift follow: lower-timeframe confirmation. | Displacement dan market structure shift menyusul: pengesahan timeframe rendah. |
| 4 | In the New York window, price reaches the objective. | Dalam tetingkap New York, harga mencapai objektif. |
| 5 | Notice the order of reasoning. The bias came first, from structure and liquidity. The Kill Zone only told us when to watch for evidence. A Kill Zone does not determine the Daily Bias by itself. | Perhatikan susunan penaakulan. Bias datang dahulu, daripada struktur dan liquidity. Kill Zone hanya memberitahu bila untuk memerhati bukti. Kill Zone tidak menentukan Daily Bias dengan sendirinya. |

## SCENE 8 · SUMMARY

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | To summarise. Map all liquidity first, and separate internal from external. | Sebagai rumusan. Petakan semua liquidity dahulu, dan asingkan internal daripada external. |
