## SCENE 2 · 7.5 · WHAT IS A JUDAS SWING

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A Judas Swing is an ICT interpretation of an initial, counter-directional move early in a session, that runs liquidity before price moves in the direction of the Daily Bias. | Judas Swing ialah tafsiran ICT bagi pergerakan awal yang bertentangan arah pada awal sesi, yang menyapu liquidity sebelum harga bergerak mengikut arah Daily Bias. |
| 2 | The name suggests betrayal: the early move misleads traders who follow it. | Namanya membayangkan pengkhianatan: pergerakan awal itu mengelirukan trader yang mengikutinya. |
| 3 | It is session-based. The London and New York opens are where it is studied most, often measured from the New York midnight open. | Ia berasaskan sesi. Pembukaan London dan New York ialah tempat ia paling banyak dikaji, sering diukur dari midnight open New York. |
| 4 | It usually involves a liquidity sweep. | Ia biasanya melibatkan sapuan liquidity. |
| 5 | It requires a Daily Bias set before the move. Otherwise, “counter-directional” has no meaning. | Ia memerlukan Daily Bias yang ditetapkan sebelum pergerakan berlaku. Jika tidak, “bertentangan arah” tidak bermakna. |
| 6 | And it is confirmed only later, by displacement and structure in the bias direction. | Dan ia hanya disahkan kemudian, melalui displacement dan struktur ke arah bias. |
| 7 | Like manipulation, the term describes price behaviour. It is not proof of deliberate manipulation by anyone. | Seperti manipulation, istilah ini menerangkan tingkah laku harga. Ia bukan bukti manipulasi yang disengajakan oleh sesiapa. |

## SCENE 3 · 7.5 · BULLISH JUDAS SWING

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The bullish Judas Swing model. Step one: the Daily Bias is bullish, established before the session. | Model Judas Swing bullish. Langkah satu: Daily Bias ialah bullish, ditetapkan sebelum sesi. |
| 2 | Step two: identify the references. The New York midnight open, and the Asian range. | Langkah dua: kenal pasti rujukan. Midnight open New York, dan range Asia. |
| 3 | Step three: at the London open, price initially moves down, below the midnight open. | Langkah tiga: pada pembukaan London, harga mula bergerak turun, di bawah midnight open. |
| 4 | Step four: it sweeps sell-side liquidity below the Asian low. This is only a potential Judas Swing at this point. | Langkah empat: ia menyapu sell-side liquidity di bawah Asian low. Pada tahap ini ia hanya potensi Judas Swing. |
| 5 | Step five: the downward move fails to sustain. Price stops making progress lower. | Langkah lima: pergerakan turun gagal bertahan. Harga berhenti membuat kemajuan ke bawah. |
| 6 | Step six: bullish displacement develops, back above the midnight open. | Langkah enam: bullish displacement terbentuk, kembali di atas midnight open. |
| 7 | Step seven: a possible bullish market structure shift above the last lower high. | Langkah tujuh: kemungkinan bullish market structure shift di atas lower high terakhir. |
| 8 | Step eight: price moves toward buy-side liquidity, here the previous day high. Only now, with context and confirmation, does the Judas Swing label fit. | Langkah lapan: harga bergerak ke arah buy-side liquidity, di sini previous day high. Hanya sekarang, dengan konteks dan pengesahan, label Judas Swing sesuai digunakan. |

## SCENE 5 · 7.5 · SESSION TIMING

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Judas Swings are usually studied around specific times. The New York midnight open is midnight New York time: noon in Malaysia during daylight time, or one p m during standard time. | Judas Swing biasanya dikaji di sekitar waktu tertentu. Midnight open New York ialah tengah malam waktu New York: tengah hari di Malaysia semasa waktu daylight, atau 1 petang semasa waktu standard. |
| 2 | The London window, two to five a m New York, is two to five p m in Malaysia during EDT, and three to six p m during EST. | Tetingkap London, 2 hingga 5 pagi New York, ialah 2 hingga 5 petang di Malaysia semasa EDT, dan 3 hingga 6 petang semasa EST. |
| 3 | The New York morning window is seven to ten p m Malaysia time during EDT, and eight to eleven p m during EST. | Tetingkap pagi New York ialah 7 hingga 10 malam waktu Malaysia semasa EDT, dan 8 hingga 11 malam semasa EST. |
| 4 | But timing is context. These are where false moves are most often studied, not where they must occur. Many sessions open without any Judas Swing at all. | Tetapi masa ialah konteks. Di sinilah pergerakan palsu paling kerap dikaji, bukan di mana ia mesti berlaku. Banyak sesi dibuka tanpa sebarang Judas Swing. |

## SCENE 6 · 7.5 · JUDAS SWING AND DAILY BIAS

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Here is the relationship with Daily Bias. On the left, the daily chart sets a bullish bias, with buy-side liquidity as the draw. | Ini hubungan dengan Daily Bias. Di sebelah kiri, carta harian menetapkan bias bullish, dengan buy-side liquidity sebagai draw. |
| 2 | On the right, the session’s first move goes the other way. Price falls below the midnight open and takes sell-side liquidity. | Di sebelah kanan, pergerakan pertama sesi itu bergerak ke arah bertentangan. Harga jatuh di bawah midnight open dan mengambil sell-side liquidity. |
| 3 | The initial move creates the liquidity, and the better price, before the expected direction. | 4 | In a bearish bias, everything inverts: the first move goes up, takes buy-side liquidity, then fails. | Dalam bias bearish, semuanya terbalik: pergerakan pertama naik, mengambil buy-side liquidity, kemudian gagal. |

## SCENE 7 · 7.5 · JUDAS SWING VS LIQUIDITY SWEEP

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | This comparison is mandatory. A liquidity sweep is simply price trading through a relevant high or low. | Perbandingan ini wajib. Sapuan liquidity hanyalah harga yang didagangkan melepasi high atau low yang relevan. |
| 2 | It can happen in any context, and by itself it does not imply a reversal. | Ia boleh berlaku dalam apa jua konteks, dan dengan sendirinya ia tidak membayangkan pembalikan. |
| 3 | It does not require any session model. | Ia tidak memerlukan sebarang model sesi. |
| 4 | A Judas Swing is an ICT-specific interpretation of an initial deceptive move. | Judas Swing ialah tafsiran khusus ICT bagi pergerakan awal yang mengelirukan. |
| 5 | It is discussed within session-based delivery, and it often involves a sweep. | Ia dibincangkan dalam konteks price delivery berasaskan sesi, dan ia sering melibatkan sapuan. |
| 6 | But it also needs a Daily Bias, relevant timing, and later confirmation. | Tetapi ia juga memerlukan Daily Bias, masa yang relevan, dan pengesahan kemudian. |
| 7 | So: a Judas Swing may involve a liquidity sweep, but not every liquidity sweep is a Judas Swing. | Jadi: Judas Swing mungkin melibatkan sapuan liquidity, tetapi bukan setiap sapuan liquidity ialah Judas Swing. |

## SCENE 8 · 7.5 · JUDAS SWING VS FALSE BREAKOUT

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How does a Judas Swing differ from a classic false breakout? They look similar: a level breaks, then price returns. | Bagaimana Judas Swing berbeza daripada false breakout klasik? Kedua-duanya kelihatan serupa: satu paras pecah, kemudian harga kembali. |
| 2 | The methodology differs. False breakouts, or bull and bear traps, belong to general technical analysis. Judas Swing belongs to ICT’s session-based model. | Metodologinya berbeza. False breakout, atau bull trap dan bear trap, tergolong dalam analisis teknikal umum. Judas Swing tergolong dalam model ICT berasaskan sesi. |
| 3 | Liquidity is central to a Judas Swing. Which liquidity was run? | Liquidity adalah teras bagi Judas Swing. Liquidity manakah yang disapu? |
| 4 | Timing matters: it happens early in a session, measured from a defined open. | Masa penting: ia berlaku pada awal sesi, diukur dari pembukaan yang ditakrifkan. |
| 5 | A Daily Bias is required, because the move must be counter to it. | Daily Bias diperlukan, kerana pergerakan itu mesti bertentangan dengannya. |
| 6 | And what follows matters: displacement and expansion in the bias direction. So, not every false breakout is a Judas Swing. | Dan apa yang menyusul penting: displacement dan pengembangan ke arah bias. Jadi, bukan setiap false breakout ialah Judas Swing. |

## SCENE 9 · 7.5 · FAILED JUDAS SWINGS

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Now the failures. Failure one: the initial move sweeps liquidity and simply continues in the same direction. | Kini kegagalan. Kegagalan satu: pergerakan awal menyapu liquidity dan terus bergerak ke arah yang sama. |
| 2 | Failure two: the sweep happens, but no displacement follows. Price drifts sideways. | Kegagalan dua: sapuan berlaku, tetapi tiada displacement yang menyusul. Harga bergerak mendatar. |
| 3 | Failure three: price bounces, but never breaks the last lower high. No meaningful structure shift develops, and the decline resumes. | Kegagalan tiga: harga melantun, tetapi tidak pernah memecah lower high terakhir. Tiada anjakan struktur yang bermakna, dan penurunan bersambung. |
| 4 | There are two more. Price can remain in consolidation all session, or the assumed Daily Bias itself can become invalid. | Ada dua lagi. Harga boleh kekal dalam konsolidasi sepanjang sesi, atau Daily Bias yang diandaikan itu sendiri boleh menjadi tidak sah. |
| 5 | This is why the initial sweep should never be treated as an entry signal by itself. | Inilah sebabnya sapuan awal tidak sepatutnya dianggap sebagai isyarat kemasukan dengan sendirinya. |

## SCENE 10 · 7.5 · COMMON MISTAKES

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Common mistakes. Calling every liquidity sweep a Judas Swing. | Kesilapan biasa. Menggelar setiap sapuan liquidity sebagai Judas Swing. |
| 2 | Ignoring session timing. | Mengabaikan masa sesi. |
| 3 | Ignoring the Daily Bias. | Mengabaikan Daily Bias. |
| 4 | Entering before there is evidence of a directional shift. | Memasuki dagangan sebelum ada bukti anjakan arah. |
| 5 | Assuming every initial move is deceptive. Many first moves are genuine. | Menganggap setiap pergerakan awal mengelirukan. Banyak pergerakan pertama adalah tulen. |
| 6 | Treating the concept as proof of institutional manipulation. | Menganggap konsep ini sebagai bukti manipulasi institusi. |
| 7 | And ignoring alternative explanations, such as news, or a genuine change in sentiment. | Dan mengabaikan penjelasan alternatif, seperti berita, atau perubahan sentimen yang tulen. |
