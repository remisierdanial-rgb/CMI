## SCENE 2 · 7.1 · BULLISH CASE (00:21–01:27)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Here is the bullish case in full. We begin with the daily open at four thousand two hundred and sixty-eight. | Ini kes bullish secara penuh. Kita mulakan dengan daily open pada 4,268. |
| 2 | Price builds an accumulation range around the open. We mark the range high and the range low. | Harga membina range accumulation di sekitar harga pembukaan. Kita tandakan range high dan range low. |
| 3 | Two touches of the low leave equal lows. Sell-side liquidity is assumed to rest beneath them. | Dua sentuhan pada low meninggalkan equal lows. Sell-side liquidity diandaikan berada di bawahnya. |
| 4 | Then price drops, below the open and through the range low. The sweep is visible: the wick trades clearly beneath the level. | Kemudian harga jatuh, di bawah harga pembukaan dan melepasi range low. Sapuan itu jelas: sumbunya didagangkan di bawah paras tersebut. |
| 5 | Next comes the reclaim. A strong bullish candle closes back above the range low. | Seterusnya berlaku reclaim. Satu candle bullish yang kuat ditutup semula di atas range low. |
| 6 | Displacement follows, and the move leaves a bullish fair value gap. | Displacement menyusul, dan pergerakan itu meninggalkan bullish fair value gap. |
| 7 | Price closes above the last lower high, a market structure shift, and then above the range high, a break of structure. | Harga ditutup di atas lower high terakhir, iaitu market structure shift, dan kemudian di atas range high, iaitu break of structure. |
| 8 | Now the distribution phase. Price expands upward. | Kini fasa distribution. Harga berkembang ke atas. |
| 9 | It runs toward buy-side liquidity above the old high, and trades through it. | Ia bergerak ke arah buy-side liquidity di atas high lama, dan didagangkan melepasinya. |
| 10 | Every step is in order and each one is visible on the chart. That is what makes this a complete illustration. | Setiap langkah berlaku mengikut urutan dan setiap satu boleh dilihat pada carta. Itulah yang menjadikan ini ilustrasi yang lengkap. |

## SCENE 3 · 7.1 · VALID VS INCOMPLETE (01:27–02:11)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What separates a valid illustration from an incomplete formation? First, a clear reference, and identifiable range boundaries. | Apakah yang membezakan ilustrasi yang sah daripada formasi yang tidak lengkap? Pertama, rujukan yang jelas, dan sempadan range yang boleh dikenal pasti. |
| 2 | Second, a sweep that genuinely trades through the level, followed by a reclaim and displacement. | Kedua, sapuan yang benar-benar didagangkan melepasi paras, diikuti oleh reclaim dan displacement. |
| 3 | Third, a structure shift in the new direction, and expansion away from the range. | Ketiga, anjakan struktur ke arah baharu, dan pengembangan menjauhi range. |
| 4 | An incomplete formation is missing one or more of these. Perhaps there is no real range, or the so-called sweep never crossed the level. | Formasi yang tidak lengkap kekurangan satu atau lebih elemen ini. Mungkin tiada range sebenar, atau sapuan yang disebut itu tidak pernah melepasi paras. |
| 5 | Perhaps price never reclaims, or no displacement or structure shift appears. | Mungkin harga tidak pernah reclaim, atau tiada displacement atau anjakan struktur yang muncul. |
| 6 | And be honest about hindsight. Labels drawn only after the move are not analysis. | Dan jujurlah tentang hindsight. Label yang dilukis hanya selepas pergerakan berlaku bukanlah analisis. |

## SCENE 5 · 7.1 · FAILED MODELS 1–2 (03:00–03:37)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Failure one. Price sweeps the range low, but it never reclaims. | Kegagalan satu. Harga menyapu range low, tetapi tidak pernah reclaim. |
| 2 | It keeps closing below the level and falls further. The bullish reading was simply wrong. The sweep was a breakdown. | Ia terus ditutup di bawah paras itu dan jatuh lebih jauh. Tafsiran bullish itu salah. Sapuan tersebut sebenarnya breakdown. |
| 3 | Failure two. Price breaks out of the range and continues in the breakout direction. | Kegagalan dua. Harga keluar daripada range dan terus bergerak ke arah breakout tersebut. |
| 4 | There is no false move at all. Someone waiting for manipulation first would miss the move, or worse, trade against it. | Tiada pergerakan palsu langsung. Sesiapa yang menunggu manipulation dahulu akan terlepas pergerakan ini, atau lebih teruk, berdagang melawannya. |
| 5 | In both cases, the sweep or breakout was only a possible manipulation until reversal evidence appeared, and it never did. | Dalam kedua-dua kes, sapuan atau breakout itu hanyalah kemungkinan manipulation sehingga bukti pembalikan muncul, dan ia tidak pernah muncul. |

## SCENE 6 · 7.1 · FAILED MODELS 3–4 (03:37–04:10)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Failure three. The sweep happens and price recovers a little, but it simply goes back to ranging. | Kegagalan tiga. Sapuan berlaku dan harga pulih sedikit, tetapi ia kembali bergerak dalam range. |
| 2 | There is no meaningful distribution phase. No expansion, no new structure. | Tiada fasa distribution yang bermakna. Tiada pengembangan, tiada struktur baharu. |
| 3 | Failure four. Price expands briefly, then falls back into the original range. | Kegagalan empat. Harga berkembang seketika, kemudian jatuh semula ke dalam range asal. |

| 5 | In both cases, the sequence is incomplete. Manage the idea as failed, rather than stretching the labels to fit. | Dalam kedua-dua kes, urutannya tidak lengkap. Uruskan idea itu sebagai gagal, dan jangan regangkan label supaya kelihatan sesuai. |

## SCENE 7 · 7.1 · FAILED MODEL 5 (04:10–04:37)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Failure five: price forms a different structure altogether. | Kegagalan lima: harga membentuk struktur yang langsung berbeza. |
| 2 | Here it sweeps the range low, then sweeps the range high as well. | Di sini ia menyapu range low, kemudian menyapu range high juga. |
| 3 | Neither move is followed by a sustained expansion. | Kedua-dua pergerakan tidak diikuti oleh pengembangan yang berterusan. |
| 4 | This is a widening or rotating range, not a Power of Three sequence. | Ini ialah range yang melebar atau berputar, bukan urutan Power of Three. |
| 5 | The professional response is to stand aside and re-read the structure, instead of forcing a PO3 label onto it. | Respons profesional ialah berdiri di tepi dan membaca semula struktur, bukannya memaksa label PO3 ke atasnya. |
