## SCENE 2 · 7.3 · WHAT IS A KILL ZONE (00:22–01:12)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | An ICT Kill Zone is a time window, stated in New York local time, that the methodology highlights for monitoring price delivery. | ICT Kill Zone ialah tetingkap masa, dinyatakan dalam waktu tempatan New York, yang ditekankan oleh metodologi ini untuk memantau price delivery. |
| 2 | Time matters because participation changes through the day, as major financial centres open and close. | Masa penting kerana penyertaan pasaran berubah sepanjang hari, apabila pusat kewangan utama dibuka dan ditutup. |
| 3 | Session opens can bring new orders, scheduled news and runs on liquidity. | Pembukaan sesi boleh membawa pesanan baharu, berita berjadual dan larian ke atas liquidity. |
| 4 | But a Kill Zone is not an official exchange trading period. Exchanges define trading hours; ICT educators define Kill Zones. | Tetapi Kill Zone bukan tempoh dagangan rasmi bursa. Bursa menentukan waktu dagangan; pendidik ICT menentukan Kill Zones. |
| 5 | It is also not a guaranteed opportunity. Nothing has to happen because a window has opened. | Ia juga bukan peluang yang dijamin. Tiada apa yang perlu berlaku hanya kerana satu tetingkap telah dibuka. |
| 6 | And it is not a universal law of markets. It is a convention inside one methodology. | Dan ia bukan hukum pasaran sejagat. Ia satu konvensyen dalam satu metodologi. |
| 7 | Think of it this way: the clock tells you when to look. Price tells you what is actually happening. | Fikirkan begini: jam memberitahu bila untuk melihat. Harga memberitahu apa yang sebenarnya berlaku. |

## SCENE 5 · 7.3 · EST AND EDT (02:31–03:34)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Malaysia Time is UTC plus eight all year. Malaysia does not observe daylight saving. | Waktu Malaysia ialah UTC+8 sepanjang tahun. Malaysia tidak mengamalkan daylight saving. |
| 2 | New York Standard Time, EST, is UTC minus five. During EST, Malaysia is thirteen hours ahead of New York. | New York Standard Time, EST, ialah UTC−5. Semasa EST, Malaysia 13 jam di hadapan New York. |
| 3 | New York Daylight Time, EDT, is UTC minus four. During EDT, Malaysia is twelve hours ahead. | New York Daylight Time, EDT, ialah UTC−4. Semasa EDT, Malaysia 12 jam di hadapan. |
| 4 | In twenty twenty-six, daylight time runs from Sunday the eighth of March to Sunday the first of November. In twenty twenty-seven, from the fourteenth of March to the seventh of November. | Pada tahun 2026, waktu daylight bermula Ahad 8 Mac hingga Ahad 1 November. Pada tahun 2027, dari 14 Mac hingga 7 November. |
| 5 | So on the fifteenth of January twenty twenty-six, two a m in New York is three p m in Malaysia. | Jadi pada 15 Januari 2026, 2 pagi di New York ialah 3 petang di Malaysia. |
| 6 | But on the fifteenth of July, two a m in New York is two p m in Malaysia. Same Kill Zone, different Malaysia time. | Tetapi pada 15 Julai, 2 pagi di New York ialah 2 petang di Malaysia. Kill Zone yang sama, waktu Malaysia yang berbeza. |
| 7 | These dates come from the IANA time zone database. Always check the date before you convert. | Tarikh-tarikh ini diambil daripada pangkalan data zon waktu IANA. Sentiasa semak tarikh sebelum anda menukar waktu. |

## SCENE 7 · 7.3 · A SUBTLE TRAP (04:14–04:54)

| 2 | Before the eighth of March, two a m in New York is seven a m in London. | Sebelum 8 Mac, 2 pagi di New York ialah 7 pagi di London. |
