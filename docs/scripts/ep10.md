## SCENE 2 · 7.6 · WHAT IS SMT (00:22–01:07)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SMT Divergence is a relative comparison of swing highs and lows between related instruments, over the same period. | SMT Divergence ialah perbandingan relatif swing high dan low antara instrumen yang berkaitan, dalam tempoh yang sama. |
| 2 | In ICT terminology, SMT stands for Smart Money Technique. | Dalam terminologi ICT, SMT bermaksud Smart Money Technique. |
| 3 | Related markets usually move together. When they reach a swing point together, we expect similar behaviour. | Pasaran yang berkaitan biasanya bergerak bersama. Apabila ia mencapai titik swing bersama, kita menjangkakan tingkah laku yang serupa. |
| 4 | When one makes a new extreme and the other does not, that disagreement is information about relative strength and weakness. | Apabila satu membuat ekstrem baharu dan yang satu lagi tidak, percanggahan itu ialah maklumat tentang kekuatan dan kelemahan relatif. |
| 5 | This is not RSI or MACD divergence. No indicator is involved. | Ini bukan divergence RSI atau MACD. Tiada indikator yang terlibat. |
| 6 | And it is an analytical comparison. It does not prove institutional activity, and it does not predict a reversal on its own. | Dan ia perbandingan analisis. Ia tidak membuktikan aktiviti institusi, dan tidak meramalkan pembalikan dengan sendirinya. |

## SCENE 3 · 7.6 · SELECTING INSTRUMENTS (01:07–01:47)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SMT is only as good as the pair. First, do they share a similar underlying exposure, and a real economic relationship? | SMT hanya sebaik pasangan yang dipilih. Pertama, adakah ia berkongsi pendedahan asas yang serupa, dan hubungan ekonomi yang sebenar? |
| 2 | Second, do they have a history of moving together? Check it; do not assume it. | Kedua, adakah ia mempunyai sejarah bergerak bersama? Semak; jangan andaikan. |
| 3 | Third, do they trade at the same time? Different session hours create false divergences. | Ketiga, adakah ia didagangkan pada masa yang sama? Waktu sesi yang berbeza mencipta divergence palsu. |
| 4 | Fourth, are the data, contract specifications and currencies comparable? | Keempat, adakah data, spesifikasi kontrak dan mata wang boleh dibandingkan? |
| 5 | Fifth, what drivers do they share, and where do they differ? | Kelima, apakah pemacu yang dikongsi, dan di mana ia berbeza? |
| 6 | And remember, correlation is not constant. Never compare unrelated instruments just because their charts look alike. | Dan ingat, korelasi tidak tetap. Jangan sekali-kali membandingkan instrumen yang tidak berkaitan hanya kerana cartanya kelihatan serupa. |

## SCENE 4 · 7.6 · BULLISH SMT (01:47–02:42)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bullish SMT. Two related instruments, on the same timeframe and the same time axis. Both have made a first swing low. | SMT bullish. Dua instrumen berkaitan, pada timeframe yang sama dan paksi masa yang sama. Kedua-duanya telah membuat swing low pertama. |
| 2 | Instrument A falls again and trades below its first low. It makes a lower low, sweeping the sell-side liquidity beneath. | Instrumen A jatuh lagi dan didagangkan di bawah low pertamanya. Ia membuat lower low, menyapu sell-side liquidity di bawahnya. |
| 3 | At the same time, Instrument B fails to make a corresponding lower low. It holds a higher low. | Pada masa yang sama, Instrumen B gagal membuat lower low yang sepadan. Ia mengekalkan higher low. |
| 4 | That disagreement is the bullish SMT Divergence. B is showing relative strength, a warning that the bearish move may be weakening. | Percanggahan itulah SMT Divergence bullish. B menunjukkan kekuatan relatif, satu amaran bahawa pergerakan bearish mungkin semakin lemah. |
| 5 | Further confirmation is still needed. Here, Instrument A later shifts structure, closing above its last lower high. | Pengesahan lanjut masih diperlukan. Di sini, Instrumen A kemudian mengalihkan struktur, ditutup di atas lower high terakhirnya. |
| 6 | Definitions vary on which instrument leads. The CMI convention: A is the instrument that makes the new extreme, B fails to confirm, and we look for confirmation on A. | Takrif berbeza tentang instrumen mana yang mendahului. Konvensyen CMI: A ialah instrumen yang membuat ekstrem baharu, B gagal mengesahkan, dan kita mencari pengesahan pada A. |

## SCENE 5 · 7.6 · BEARISH SMT (02:42–03:16)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bearish SMT is the opposite. Both instruments make a first swing high. | SMT bearish ialah sebaliknya. Kedua-dua instrumen membuat swing high pertama. |
| 2 | Instrument A rallies above its first high, a higher high. Instrument B fails to follow, and prints a lower high. | Instrumen A naik melepasi high pertamanya, iaitu higher high. Instrumen B gagal mengikut, dan mencetak lower high. |
| 3 | That is bearish SMT Divergence: relative weakness, a warning that the bullish move may be losing strength. | Itulah SMT Divergence bearish: kelemahan relatif, satu amaran bahawa pergerakan bullish mungkin kehilangan kekuatan. |
| 4 | Again, confirmation is needed. Here Instrument A later shifts structure lower. | Sekali lagi, pengesahan diperlukan. Di sini Instrumen A kemudian mengalihkan struktur ke bawah. |
| 5 | Without that confirmation, the divergence is only an observation. | Tanpa pengesahan itu, divergence hanyalah pemerhatian. |

## SCENE 6 · 7.6 · SMT AND LIQUIDITY (03:16–03:46)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SMT connects naturally with Level Three liquidity. Both instruments have sell-side liquidity under a previous low. | SMT berkait secara semula jadi dengan liquidity Level 3. Kedua-dua instrumen mempunyai sell-side liquidity di bawah low sebelumnya. |
| 2 | Instrument A sweeps it. | Instrumen A menyapunya. |
| 3 | Instrument B does not. Its sell-side liquidity remains intact. | Instrumen B tidak. Sell-side liquidity-nya kekal utuh. |
| 4 | The same applies with buy-side liquidity in bearish SMT. But evaluate it within the broader market. Bukan setiap ketidakpadanan antara dua high atau low ialah SMT yang bermakna. |

## SCENE 7 · 7.6 · SMT AND STRUCTURE (03:46–04:17)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Now link SMT with Level Two structure. On Instrument A, the bearish leg prints a lower high and a lower low. | Kini kaitkan SMT dengan struktur Level 2. Pada Instrumen A, kaki bearish mencetak lower high dan lower low. |
| 2 | The SMT divergence appears at that lower low, because Instrument B holds its low. | Divergence SMT muncul pada lower low tersebut, kerana Instrumen B mengekalkan low-nya. |
| 3 | Structure then confirms with a market structure shift above the lower high. | Struktur kemudian mengesahkan dengan market structure shift di atas lower high. |
| 4 | A new higher high would extend the shift. | Higher high baharu akan memanjangkan anjakan tersebut. |
| 5 | SMT adds context around a possible shift. It does not replace structure analysis. | SMT menambah konteks di sekitar kemungkinan anjakan. Ia tidak menggantikan analisis struktur. |

## SCENE 9 · 7.6 · SMT VS OSCILLATOR DIVERGENCE (04:47–05:33)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How does SMT differ from RSI or MACD divergence? SMT compares one instrument’s price with a related instrument’s price. Oscillator divergence compares price with an indicator calculated from that same price. | Bagaimana SMT berbeza daripada divergence RSI atau MACD? SMT membandingkan harga satu instrumen dengan harga instrumen berkaitan. Divergence oscillator membandingkan harga dengan indikator yang dikira daripada harga yang sama. |
| 2 | SMT uses relative highs and lows across instruments. Oscillator divergence uses price extremes versus indicator extremes. | SMT menggunakan high dan low relatif merentas instrumen. Divergence oscillator menggunakan ekstrem harga berbanding ekstrem indikator. |
| 3 | SMT depends on the relationship between instruments. Oscillator divergence depends on the indicator formula and settings. | SMT bergantung pada hubungan antara instrumen. Divergence oscillator bergantung pada formula dan tetapan indikator. |
| 4 | SMT highlights relative strength or weakness. Oscillator divergence highlights momentum. | SMT menonjolkan kekuatan atau kelemahan relatif. Divergence oscillator menonjolkan momentum. |
| 5 | They are separate techniques, with separate failure modes. | Kedua-duanya teknik yang berasingan, dengan cara kegagalan yang berasingan. |

## SCENE 10 · 7.6 · COMMON MISTAKES (05:33–06:07)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Common mistakes. Comparing unrelated instruments. | Kesilapan biasa. Membandingkan instrumen yang tidak berkaitan. |
| 2 | Using different or misaligned timeframes. | Menggunakan timeframe yang berbeza atau tidak sejajar. |
| 3 | Ignoring session differences, and comparing data that is not synchronised. | Mengabaikan perbezaan sesi, dan membandingkan data yang tidak disegerakkan. |
| 4 | Assuming correlation is permanent. | Menganggap korelasi adalah kekal. |
| 5 | Treating every mismatch as SMT. | Menganggap setiap ketidakpadanan sebagai SMT. |
| 6 | Treating SMT as an automatic reversal signal. | Menganggap SMT sebagai isyarat pembalikan automatik. |
| 7 | Confusing SMT with RSI or MACD divergence. | Mengelirukan SMT dengan divergence RSI atau MACD. |
| 8 | And ignoring the broader market structure. | Dan mengabaikan struktur pasaran yang lebih luas. |

## SCENE 11 · SUMMARY (06:07–06:34)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | To summarise. SMT compares the swings of related instruments, on the same timeframe, the same time axis, and the same swing. | Sebagai rumusan. SMT membandingkan swing instrumen yang berkaitan, pada timeframe yang sama, paksi masa yang sama, dan swing yang sama. |
| 2 | Bullish: A makes a lower low while B holds a higher low. Bearish: A makes a higher high while B makes a lower high. | Bullish: A membuat lower low manakala B mengekalkan higher low. Bearish: A membuat higher high manakala B membuat lower high. |
