## SCENE 2 · 7.4 · WHAT IS DAILY BIAS

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily Bias is a directional working hypothesis for the day. | Daily Bias ialah hipotesis kerja berarah bagi hari tersebut. |
| 2 | A bullish bias expects the day to favour higher prices and buy-side objectives. A bearish bias expects the opposite. | Bias bullish menjangkakan hari itu memihak kepada harga lebih tinggi dan objektif buy-side. Bias bearish menjangkakan sebaliknya. |
| 3 | A neutral bias means there is no clear directional edge. That is a legitimate answer. | Bias neutral bermaksud tiada kelebihan arah yang jelas. Itu jawapan yang sah. |
| 4 | We set bias before intraday analysis because it frames which setups we study, and which we ignore. | Kita tetapkan bias sebelum analisis intraday kerana ia menentukan setup mana yang kita kaji, dan mana yang kita abaikan. |
| 5 | But bias is not a guaranteed prediction. It is a hypothesis that should be updated when conditions change. | Tetapi bias bukan ramalan yang dijamin. Ia hipotesis yang perlu dikemas kini apabila keadaan berubah. |
| 6 | And bias is not confirmation. A directional expectation still needs lower-timeframe evidence before any trade decision. | Dan bias bukan pengesahan. Jangkaan arah masih memerlukan bukti timeframe lebih rendah sebelum sebarang keputusan dagangan. |

## SCENE 4 · 7.4 · DRAW ON LIQUIDITY

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Step two is the Draw on Liquidity: the liquidity pool price is judged most likely to move toward next. | Langkah kedua ialah Draw on Liquidity: kolam liquidity yang dinilai paling mungkin menjadi arah harga seterusnya. |
| 2 | Above, buy-side liquidity rests beyond the old high. This is external liquidity, at the edge of the range. | Di atas, buy-side liquidity berada di luar high lama. Ini external liquidity, di tepi range. |
| 3 | Below, sell-side liquidity rests beneath the last swing low. Also external. | Di bawah, sell-side liquidity berada di bawah swing low terakhir. Juga external. |
| 4 | Inside the range, the fair value gap is internal liquidity, a place price may revisit along the way. | Di dalam range, fair value gap ialah internal liquidity, tempat yang mungkin dikunjungi semula oleh harga dalam perjalanan. |
| 5 | With bullish structure and price retracing, a reasonable draw is the buy-side liquidity above the old high. | Dengan struktur bullish dan harga sedang retrace, draw yang munasabah ialah buy-side liquidity di atas high lama. |
| 6 | But a Draw on Liquidity is an analytical expectation, not a guaranteed destination. | Tetapi Draw on Liquidity ialah jangkaan analisis, bukan destinasi yang dijamin. |

## SCENE 5 · 7.4 · LIQUIDITY TYPES

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which levels are candidates for the draw? The previous day high and low are the most common intraday objectives. | Paras manakah calon bagi draw? Previous day high dan low ialah objektif intraday yang paling lazim. |
| 2 | The previous week high and low are larger objectives that may take several days. | Previous week high dan low ialah objektif yang lebih besar dan mungkin mengambil beberapa hari. |
| 3 | Equal highs and equal lows, but only where they are genuinely equal. | Equal highs dan equal lows, tetapi hanya di mana ia benar-benar sama. |
| 4 | Old swing highs and lows at the edges of the dealing range are external liquidity. | Swing high dan low lama di tepi dealing range ialah external liquidity. |
| 5 | And imbalances inside the range are internal liquidity, which price may rebalance before reaching the external target. | Dan ketidakseimbangan di dalam range ialah internal liquidity, yang mungkin diseimbangkan semula oleh harga sebelum mencapai sasaran luaran. |

## SCENE 6 · 7.4 · DEALING RANGE & LOCATION

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|

| 2 | Equilibrium is the fifty percent level. Below it is discount; above it is premium. | Equilibrium ialah paras 50 peratus. Di bawahnya ialah discount; di atasnya ialah premium. |
| 3 | The optimal trade entry zone sits between the sixty-two and seventy-nine percent retracement. Price is trading inside it now. | Zon optimal trade entry berada antara retracement 62 dan 79 peratus. Harga kini didagangkan di dalamnya. |
| 4 | Just below price is a bullish fair value gap, a bullish PD array. | Tepat di bawah harga terdapat bullish fair value gap, iaitu bullish PD array. |
| 5 | Bullish structure with price in discount, approaching a bullish PD array, supports the bullish hypothesis. | Struktur bullish dengan harga dalam discount, menghampiri bullish PD array, menyokong hipotesis bullish. |
| 6 | If the same structure were trading in premium, into a bearish array, the bullish case would be weaker. And if the daily and intraday locations conflict, confidence drops further. | Jika struktur yang sama didagangkan dalam premium, ke arah bearish array, kes bullish akan menjadi lebih lemah. Dan jika lokasi harian dan intraday bercanggah, keyakinan menurun lagi. |

## SCENE 9 · 7.4 · WHEN THE BULLISH BIAS FAILS

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Now the alternative. The sell-side sweep happens, just as before. | Kini alternatifnya. Sapuan sell-side berlaku, sama seperti sebelum ini. |
| 2 | But there is no reclaim, no displacement, and no structure shift. Price keeps falling. | Tetapi tiada reclaim, tiada displacement, dan tiada anjakan struktur. Harga terus jatuh. |
| 3 | It breaks the invalidation level you wrote down before the session, for example the failure of the discount array or the higher-timeframe low. | Ia memecah paras invalidation yang anda tulis sebelum sesi, contohnya kegagalan discount array atau low timeframe tinggi. |
| 4 | The bullish bias is invalid. Update it to neutral, or re-assess for bearish conditions. Do not defend it. | Bias bullish tidak lagi sah. Kemas kini kepada neutral, atau nilai semula untuk keadaan bearish. Jangan mempertahankannya. |

## SCENE 11 · 7.4 · NEUTRAL BIAS

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sometimes the market offers no clear direction. Here, higher-timeframe structure is conflicting: a range after a sharp decline. | Kadangkala pasaran tidak menawarkan arah yang jelas. Di sini, struktur timeframe tinggi bercanggah: range selepas penurunan yang tajam. |
| 2 | Price is trapped between buy-side liquidity above the equal highs and sell-side liquidity below the equal lows. | Harga terperangkap antara buy-side liquidity di atas equal highs dan sell-side liquidity di bawah equal lows. |

| 6 | In these conditions, neutral is a valid analytical conclusion. Not having an opinion is sometimes the professional opinion. | Dalam keadaan ini, neutral ialah kesimpulan analisis yang sah. Tidak mempunyai pendapat kadangkala ialah pendapat yang profesional. |
