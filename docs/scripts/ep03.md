## SCENE 2 · 7.2 · WHAT IS AMD (00:25–01:18)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | AMD describes three phases of price behaviour around a range: a contained range, a false move beyond a boundary, and a directional expansion. | AMD menerangkan tiga fasa tingkah laku harga di sekitar range: range yang terkawal, pergerakan palsu melepasi sempadan, dan pengembangan berarah. |
| 2 | It is widely used in ICT and SMC education to read how ranges resolve. | Ia digunakan secara meluas dalam pendidikan ICT dan SMC untuk membaca cara sesuatu range diselesaikan. |
| 3 | Traders study ranges because liquidity tends to rest around their obvious highs and lows. | Trader mengkaji range kerana liquidity cenderung berada di sekitar high dan low yang jelas. |
| 4 | That is the key difference from a simple breakout pattern. AMD expects the first break to be a false move. | Itulah perbezaan utama berbanding corak breakout biasa. AMD menjangkakan pecahan pertama sebagai pergerakan palsu. |
| 5 | And because it is a conceptual framework, the market does not have to complete all three phases neatly, or at all. | Dan kerana ia rangka kerja konseptual, pasaran tidak perlu melengkapkan ketiga-tiga fasa dengan kemas, atau langsung. |
| 6 | How does AMD relate to PO3? PO3 anchors the sequence to the opening price of a period. | Bagaimana AMD berkait dengan PO3? PO3 menambat urutan kepada opening price bagi sesuatu tempoh. |
| 7 | AMD focuses on the behaviour within each phase, and on the transitions between them. | AMD pula fokus pada tingkah laku dalam setiap fasa, dan pada peralihan di antaranya. |

## SCENE 3 · 7.2 · ACCUMULATION PHASE (01:18–02:06)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phase one, accumulation. After a decline, price enters consolidation. Directional progress slows, and price moves sideways. | Fasa satu, accumulation. Selepas penurunan, harga memasuki konsolidasi. Pergerakan berarah menjadi perlahan, dan harga bergerak mendatar. |
| 2 | Repeated highs and lows define the range boundaries: the range high and the range low. | High dan low yang berulang menentukan sempadan range: range high dan range low. |
| 3 | Where two highs or two lows are genuinely equal, we mark equal highs and equal lows. Here they match to the tick. | Di mana dua high atau dua low benar-benar sama, kita tandakan equal highs dan equal lows. Di sini ia sepadan hingga ke tick. |
| 4 | Potential liquidity sits above the range and below it: buy stops above, sell stops below. | Liquidity yang berpotensi berada di atas dan di bawah range: buy stop di atas, sell stop di bawah. |
| 5 | Inside the range, price fluctuates internally. Those internal swings are not yet the main event. | Di dalam range, harga berayun secara dalaman. Ayunan dalaman itu belum lagi peristiwa utama. |
| 6 | One caution. A range is an observation. Calling it institutional accumulation is an interpretation that a chart alone cannot prove. | Satu peringatan. Range ialah pemerhatian. Menggelarnya accumulation institusi ialah tafsiran yang tidak boleh dibuktikan oleh carta semata-mata. |

## SCENE 4 · 7.2 · MANIPULATION PHASE (02:06–02:56)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phase two, manipulation. In a bullish context, price breaks below the range boundary. | Fasa dua, manipulation. Dalam konteks bullish, harga memecah ke bawah sempadan range. |
| 2 | It trades through the range low and the equal lows, sweeping the sell-side liquidity beneath them. | Ia didagangkan melepasi range low dan equal lows, menyapu sell-side liquidity di bawahnya. |
| 3 | Then it rejects and returns into the range. Traders call this a false breakout. | Kemudian ia ditolak dan kembali ke dalam range. Trader menggelarnya false breakout. |
| 4 | Notice the first, shallow break. Smart money traders often call this kind of early, obvious liquidity inducement: it tempts breakout sellers before the deeper sweep. | Perhatikan pecahan pertama yang cetek. Trader smart money sering menggelar liquidity awal yang jelas seperti ini sebagai inducement: ia memancing penjual breakout sebelum sapuan yang lebih dalam. |
| 5 | In a bearish context it is the mirror image. Price sweeps above the range high, then rejects back inside. | Dalam konteks bearish, ia gambaran cermin. Harga menyapu di atas range high, kemudian ditolak kembali ke dalam. |
| 6 | One more time, because it matters: manipulation here is an ICT label for a false move. It is not proof of illegal or coordinated market manipulation. | Sekali lagi, kerana ia penting: manipulation di sini ialah label ICT bagi pergerakan palsu. Ia bukan bukti manipulasi pasaran yang haram atau diselaraskan. |

## SCENE 5 · 7.2 · DISTRIBUTION PHASE (02:56–03:42)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phase three, distribution. It begins with strong directional candles: displacement away from the sweep. | Fasa tiga, distribution. Ia bermula dengan candle berarah yang kuat: displacement menjauhi sapuan. |
| 2 | Displacement often leaves an imbalance behind, a fair value gap. | Displacement sering meninggalkan ketidakseimbangan, iaitu fair value gap. |
| 3 | Structure develops. First a market structure shift above the last lower high, then a break above the range. | Struktur terbentuk. Mula-mula market structure shift di atas lower high terakhir, kemudian break di atas range. |
| 4 | Price moves toward external liquidity, here the old high that began the decline. | Harga bergerak ke arah external liquidity, di sini high lama yang memulakan penurunan. |
| 5 | It trades through that high. What happens next is open. | Ia didagangkan melepasi high tersebut. Apa yang berlaku seterusnya masih terbuka. |
| 6 | The move may continue, or it may exhaust. Distribution does not guarantee continuation. | Pergerakan mungkin berterusan, atau mungkin kehabisan tenaga. Distribution tidak menjamin kesinambungan. |
| 7 | And remember: AMD distribution means directional expansion. It is not the Wyckoff distribution schematic. | Dan ingat: distribution dalam AMD bermaksud pengembangan berarah. Ia bukan skema distribution Wyckoff. |

## SCENE 8 · 7.2 · AMD VS WYCKOFF (05:06–05:59)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Now a mandatory comparison. In ICT and SMC, AMD is a price-delivery framework. In Wyckoff, accumulation and distribution are broader market-cycle structures with defined phases. | Kini perbandingan wajib. Dalam ICT dan SMC, AMD ialah rangka kerja price delivery. Dalam Wyckoff, accumulation dan distribution ialah struktur kitaran pasaran yang lebih luas dengan fasa yang ditakrifkan. |
| 2 | In AMD, manipulation is a general interpretation of a false move. Wyckoff names specific events, such as the Spring and the Upthrust. | Dalam AMD, manipulation ialah tafsiran umum bagi pergerakan palsu. Wyckoff menamakan peristiwa tertentu, seperti Spring dan Upthrust. |
| 3 | AMD focuses on liquidity and price delivery. Wyckoff focuses on supply and demand, cause and effect, and effort versus result, usually with volume. | AMD fokus pada liquidity dan price delivery. Wyckoff fokus pada supply dan demand, cause and effect, serta effort versus result, biasanya dengan volume. |
| 4 | AMD is commonly applied intraday and by session. Wyckoff is often applied to broader market phases. | AMD lazimnya digunakan secara intraday dan mengikut sesi. Wyckoff sering digunakan pada fasa pasaran yang lebih luas. |
| 5 | And the word distribution means opposite things. In AMD it is the expansion phase. In Wyckoff, it is a top. | Dan perkataan distribution membawa maksud yang berbeza. Dalam AMD ia fasa pengembangan. Dalam Wyckoff, ia satu puncak. |

## SCENE 9 · 7.2 · SIMILAR SHAPE, DIFFERENT FRAMEWORK (05:59–06:32)

| # | English narration | Sari kata Bahasa Melayu |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The same price action can be read by both methods. An AMD reader sees a range, a sweep, and the start of distribution. | Pergerakan harga yang sama boleh dibaca dengan kedua-dua kaedah. Pembaca AMD melihat range, sapuan, dan permulaan distribution. |
| 2 | The evidence comes from liquidity, structure and displacement. | Buktinya datang daripada liquidity, struktur dan displacement. |
| 3 | A Wyckoff reader may see a trading range, a possible Spring in Phase C, then a test and a sign of strength. | Pembaca Wyckoff mungkin melihat trading range, kemungkinan Spring dalam Fasa C, kemudian test dan sign of strength. |
| 4 | Their evidence comes from volume and effort versus result. | Bukti mereka datang daripada volume dan effort versus result. |
| 5 | Both can be useful. But use one vocabulary at a time, and do not mix labels without saying so. | Kedua-duanya boleh berguna. Tetapi gunakan satu perbendaharaan kata pada satu masa, dan jangan campur label tanpa menyatakannya. |
